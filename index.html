Setting it up, about five minutes

  1. 1

    Open the installer

    Open Contra.Music.Suite.Setup.exe from your Downloads. It installs for you only and needs no administrator.

    Windows protected your PC? That appears for any program without a paid signing certificate. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  2. 2

    Let it add Equalizer APO

    On the last screen, leave Install Equalizer APO ticked. It is the free sound engine the Suite works through, and Windows asks your permission once.

  3. 3

    Tick your headphones, then restart

    A window called Device Selector opens. Tick the output your headphones are plugged into, often called Headphones or Speakers, choose OK, and restart the PC when it asks.

  4. 4

    Turn the volume down, then take over

    Open Contra Music Suite from the Start menu and choose Take over. Start from a preset such as Bass boost or Vocal presence, or run Find my sound to build one from what you prefer.

On your PC

  • The EQ, with presets, a band-by-band editor, and published corrections for thousands of headphone models
  • Find my sound, a blind A/B test that saves a preset from your answers
  • The music on a live chart, coloured by the album cover
  • Lyrics and guitar chords for the song playing, in Spotify or any player Windows knows
  • An audio editor for any song or recording

Not on your PC yet

  • Splitting songs into vocals, drums and bass, which runs on a PC with a strong graphics card
  • The Suite account that syncs between PCs, which is by invitation for now

Nothing you play leaves your PC. To find lyrics and album covers, a song's title and artist are looked up online.

To remove it: Settings, Apps, Contra Music Suite. Equalizer APO is listed on its own; remove it too to take the EQ out of Windows.
